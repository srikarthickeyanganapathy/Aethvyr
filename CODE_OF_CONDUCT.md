# Community conduct

People using or contributing to Aethvyr should be able to discuss playback issues, accessibility needs and engineering decisions respectfully. Give actionable feedback, credit others' work, and make room for contributors with different experience and devices.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language and sharing another person's private information are grounds for moderation. Project maintainers may remove material or limit participation to protect contributors. Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer. Security issues follow the [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).
